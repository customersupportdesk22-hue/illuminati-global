Frequently Asked Questions

How can I become a member?

Select Join the Brotherhood, complete the registration form and review the membership terms before submitting your application.

Is registration free?

Yes. Creating a standard membership account is free. No registration or initiation payment is required.

Can people from other countries join?

Yes. The community welcomes eligible applicants from around the world, subject to the membership terms and applicable laws.

What are the membership principles?

Members are expected to respect one another, maintain honesty, protect personal information, follow community guidelines and act responsibly. No violence, coercion, exploitation or harmful rituals are permitted.

Does membership provide financial benefits?

No financial benefits, profits, investments or guaranteed payouts are promised through membership.
