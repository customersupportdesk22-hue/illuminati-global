Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. Information collected

When you register, the platform stores your name, email address, phone number, selected country, country calling code, display currency, password hash and the date you accepted the terms. Passwords are hashed and are not stored in plain text.

2. Location assistance

Country detection is optional. If you choose to use it, your browser may request device location permission so the site can suggest a country. You can decline and choose your country manually. Location is not required to create an account.

3. How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, display member profiles, support the member dashboard, maintain account records and protect the service.

4. Account and ledger records

Internal balance adjustments and their notes, amounts, currency, administrator and timestamps are retained as account activity records for transparency and dispute review.

5. Security and retention

The service uses password hashing, authenticated sessions and access controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Account information is retained while needed to operate the service and meet applicable obligations.

6. Sharing

Personal information is not intended for public display. It may be processed by hosting and database providers that support the service, or disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

7. Your choices

You may choose not to use optional location detection. For access, correction or deletion requests, contact the site operator through the official contact channel published by the site.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The latest revision date appears at the top of this page.
